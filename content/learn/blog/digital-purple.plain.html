Digital Purple: A Cosmic Twist on a Classic VIVIDS Shade

By PRAVANA Education · 6 min read

Digital Purple is a cosmic twist on a classic VIVIDS shade. This look pairs a deep, dimensional purple with a soft, blended melt for a finish that feels otherworldly.

Formulas

Lightening: pre-lighten to a clean level 8/9. VIVIDS: Digital Purple mixed with a clear gloss for a softer, wearable tone. Adjust the ratio for intensity.

Application

Step 1: Sectioning. Divide the hair into four quadrants and secure each section.

Step 2: Pre-Lightening. Apply the lightener to mid-lengths and ends, then process to the desired level.

Step 3: VIVIDS Application. Apply Digital Purple from the nape forward, working in fine sections for even saturation.

Step 4: Fringe and Quadrants. Continue through the fringe and remaining quadrants, keeping the application consistent.

Step 5: Reveal. Rinse, condition, and style. The result is a rich, cosmic purple with a glossy finish.

Pro tip
Mix Digital Purple with a clear gloss to soften the tone, or with VIVIDS Original for more intensity. Always strand-test before a full application.
