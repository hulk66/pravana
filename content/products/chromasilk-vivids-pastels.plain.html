ChromaSilk VIVIDS Pastels

Create beautiful hair color with Pastels. VIVIDS Pastels offer four delicate shades that can be used alone, intermixed or combined with other VIVIDS shades for ultimate customization.

Features
The industry’s largest semi-permanent creative color collection. Intermixable with any VIVIDS shade for an array of color creations.
Format
Ready-to-use, non-oxidative color. No developer required.
Finish
Vibrant, customizable pastel shades with lasting shine.
How to Use
Apply to pre-lightened hair. Process for 20–30 minutes, then rinse thoroughly. Mix with other VIVIDS shades to customize the tone.
