Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about PRAVANA products, education, and pro membership. This is demo content for the RFP preview.

Where can I buy PRAVANA products?
PRAVANA products are available through professional distributors such as CosmoProf and SalonCentric. Use the store locator to find a salon near you.
Do I need a license to use PRAVANA color?
PRAVANA products are designed for salon professionals. Check your local licensing requirements and distributor policies.
How do I choose the right developer?
Developer choice depends on the desired lift and the color line. Refer to the product guide or ask your PRAVANA educator for a recommendation.
What is the Pro Membership?
The Pro Membership gives salon professionals access to education, assets, and gated tools. In this demo, the Pro Login button is a static placeholder.
Where can I find education and events?
Visit the Learn and Events sections for articles, how-tos, and upcoming digital events.
